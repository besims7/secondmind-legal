How to request deletion

You can request deletion without installing or opening the app.

  1. Email secondmindsupport@gmail.com from the email address registered with your SecondMind account.
  2. Use the subject “SecondMind account deletion”.
  3. State that you want your account and associated data deleted.

We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing your request. Never send your password or payment details.

Data covered by deletion

The request covers your SecondMind account, account profile, and associated data stored in our backend, including cloud backups of tasks, journal entries, wellbeing entries and personal finance records, where present.

Data stored only on your device must also be removed using the app's data controls or your device's app-storage settings.

Information that may be retained

Records required for legal, accounting, security or fraud-prevention purposes may be retained for the applicable required period. If this applies to your request, we will explain which records are retained, why, and for how long.

Google Play, Apple and other service providers may retain transaction records under their own policies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your SecondMind account does not cancel an app-store subscription. Cancel your subscription separately in Google Play or your Apple account to prevent future renewals.
