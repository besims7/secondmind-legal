Privacy Policy for SecondMind

Last updated: October 2, 2026

SecondMind ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal organization and productivity application provided by SecondMind Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use SecondMind.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you choose to use, SecondMind may process information you provide, including:

  • Account information, such as your email address;
  • Profile information you choose to enter;
  • Tasks, plans, reminders, and other productivity information;
  • Mood and wellbeing entries;
  • Journal entries;
  • Manually entered income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, and other personal finance information;
  • Information you choose to provide when using AI-related features.

2. Personal Finance Features

SecondMind includes personal money-management tools that allow users to manually record and organize information such as income, expenses, budgets, and savings goals.

SecondMind does not connect directly to your bank account, does not initiate money transfers, does not provide banking services, does not issue loans or credit, and does not execute financial transactions.

The financial tools are intended for personal organization and informational purposes and do not constitute professional financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

3. AI Features and API Keys

SecondMind may provide optional AI-assisted features. Certain full AI features require users to provide their own compatible third-party AI API key.

When a user provides an API key, it is stored using secure storage mechanisms on the user's device. SecondMind does not operate a developer-funded AI gateway for these user-provided API requests.

When you use an AI feature that communicates with a third-party AI provider, the information required to process your request may be sent to that provider. The processing of that information is also subject to the privacy terms and policies of the applicable third-party provider.

SecondMind may also provide limited local assistance features that operate without requiring a personal AI API key.

4. Data Storage and Service Providers

SecondMind may use third-party service providers to operate certain parts of the app. These may include:

  • Supabase for account, authentication, database, and backend functionality;
  • RevenueCat for subscription and entitlement management;
  • Google Play and Apple App Store for app distribution and purchase processing;
  • Third-party AI providers when a user chooses to use applicable AI features.

These providers may process limited information necessary to provide their services and operate under their own privacy policies and security practices.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

SecondMind may offer paid subscriptions. Payments are processed by the app-store platform through which the subscription is purchased, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

SecondMind Labs does not directly receive or store your full payment card details.

6. Notifications

If you grant permission, SecondMind may use device notifications for features such as task reminders and other app-related reminders. Notification permissions can be changed through your device settings.

7. How We Use Information

Information processed through SecondMind may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the app's features;
  • Authenticate and manage user accounts;
  • Save and restore user information where applicable;
  • Provide planning, organization, wellbeing, finance, and AI-assisted functionality;
  • Manage subscription access;
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate requested features, process subscriptions, maintain the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Information associated with an account may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service or as otherwise required by law.

Where available, users can request or initiate deletion of their SecondMind account through the app. Account deletion is designed to remove associated account data from SecondMind's backend systems, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record requirements.

Information stored only on a user's device may also be removed by using app-provided controls, clearing app data, or uninstalling the application, depending on the type of information.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled through SecondMind. However, no electronic storage, device, network, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Wellbeing Information

SecondMind includes wellbeing and self-organization features for informational and personal-support purposes. SecondMind is not a medical service and does not replace professional medical, psychological, or emergency care.

12. Children's Privacy

SecondMind is not intended for children under the minimum age required to independently consent to the processing of personal information under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

13. International Processing

Third-party service providers used by SecondMind may process information in countries other than the country in which you live. Where applicable, data is handled according to relevant legal and contractual requirements.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SecondMind, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be made available through the published Privacy Policy page.
